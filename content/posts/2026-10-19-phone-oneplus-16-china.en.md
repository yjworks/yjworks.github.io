A 9,000mAh battery used to describe a power bank. OnePlus put that capacity inside the OnePlus 16, announced in China on October 12, and then confirmed buyers outside China will not get it.

## Battery up from 7,300mAh, charging slower

| Spec | OnePlus 16 | OnePlus 15 |
|---|---|---|
| Chip | Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 (2nm) | Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 |
| Display | 6.78-inch AMOLED, up to 185Hz | 6.78-inch, 165Hz |
| Battery | 9,000mAh | 7,300mAh |
| Charging | 100W wired / 50W wireless | 120W wired |
| Cameras | 200MP main (OIS), 50MP 3x telephoto, 50MP ultrawide, 50MP front | — |
| China price | 12GB/256GB CNY 5,999; 12GB/512GB CNY 6,799; 16GB/512GB CNY 7,499; 16GB/1TB CNY 8,499 | — |

> **Not yet disclosed:** official weight and thickness (outlets disagree, so we left them out), the on-sale date (reports split between same-day sales and October 31), and any pricing outside China. We'll update this post when they are.

## More capacity does not guarantee more hours

Reports describe 9,000mAh as the typical capacity of a two-cell pack, not a runtime promise, and 185Hz as a ceiling that does not apply in every app. On the published numbers, the battery is about 23% larger than the OnePlus 15's, while wired charging drops from 120W to 100W. Charge times need independent testing.

## Outside China, this is an import-only phone

OnePlus has said it will stop launching new products in North America and Europe, and the OnePlus 16 is reported as China-only. We found no official word on a Korea launch or Korean pricing, and the CNY 5,999 starting price cannot be compared directly with local prices.

Sources: [Android Headlines](https://www.androidheadlines.com/2026/10/oneplus-16-is-official-and-youll-be-disappointed-you-cant-buy-it.html), [91mobiles](https://www.91mobiles.com/hub/oneplus-16-launched-china-price-specifications/)

In short, the spec sheet is strong for anyone who wants a huge battery and a flagship chip, but the lack of an official sales and service channel outside China is the deciding limit.
