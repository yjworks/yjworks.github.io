Two phones, one Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6, and a 2,000mAh gap in battery size. Motorola's new flagship is the smaller one, so what is it spending its budget on?

Motorola announced the Signature 27 on November 5 at $1,599.99 in the US, its highest US price to date. US preorders open November 19 and retail sales start November 26.

## Same chip, 2,000mAh less battery, and the camera pixels flipped

| | Motorola Signature 27 | OnePlus 16 |
|---|---|---|
| Chip | Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 | Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 (2nm) |
| Display | 6.8-inch tandem OLED, 2772×1272, 120Hz, 12,000 nits peak (Motorola figure, 1% average pixel level) | 6.78-inch AMOLED, up to 185Hz |
| Battery | 7,000mAh | 9,000mAh |
| Charging | 90W wired / 50W wireless | 100W wired / 50W wireless |
| Main camera | 50MP | 200MP |
| Telephoto | 200MP periscope, 3x optical | 50MP, 3x |
| Ultrawide / front | 50MP / 50MP | 50MP / 50MP |
| Weight | 227g | — |
| Ingress rating | IP68 / IP69 | — |
| Price | $1,599.99 (US) | From CNY 5,999 (China) |

Sources: [Techaeris](https://techaeris.com/2026/11/05/motorola-signature-27-arrives-at-1599-99/), [Tech Insider](https://tech-insider.org/motorola-signature-27-1599-flagship-specs-price-2026/), [Droid Life](https://www.droid-life.com/2026/11/05/motorola-signature-27-coming-to-us-absolute-specimen-priced-at-1599/), [our OnePlus 16 post](/en/2026/11/06/phone-oneplus-16-china/), [91mobiles](https://www.91mobiles.com/hub/oneplus-16-launched-china-price-specifications/)

> **Not yet disclosed:** Korea availability and KRW pricing, real-world battery life, and charge times. We'll update this post when they are.

On the published numbers, Motorola leaned on brightness and the telephoto rather than battery size. The 7,000mAh cell is 35% larger than the previous generation (per Techaeris) but about 22% smaller than the OnePlus 16's. The telephoto is a 200MP periscope, while the OnePlus 16 puts its 200MP on the main sensor, so the two phones split their pixel budget in opposite ways. Pixel count is not image quality; real-world comparisons are needed to rank them.

## For GrapheneOS, only the announcement is confirmed

Motorola presented the Signature 27 as its first GrapheneOS device and said more details would follow over the coming months ([Techaeris](https://techaeris.com/2026/11/05/motorola-signature-27-arrives-at-1599-99/)). An Italian outlet reported that support is not there at launch ([Techprincess](https://techprincess.it/motorola-signature-27-prezzo-uscita-italia/)), and search results indicate TechRadar called the timing unclear. We could not confirm that GrapheneOS is usable on day one, so anyone buying for that reason should wait for a published schedule. The phone ships with Android 17 and is reported to get seven years of OS updates (Droid Life adds seven years of security updates).

## Korea has no announced path yet

We found no Korean launch or price announcement. The Italian outlet reported a European starting price of €1,399, sold through Motorola's own site. Buyers in Korea would be importing, so local warranty and carrier support need to be checked separately.

In short: if you want the bigger battery and a China-market price, the OnePlus 16 leads on paper but is sold officially only in China. The Signature 27 offers official US and European sales, seven years of updates and a very bright screen, while its headline GrapheneOS feature still has no schedule.
