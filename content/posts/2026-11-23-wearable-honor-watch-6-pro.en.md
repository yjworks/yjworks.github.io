A "Pro" watch with roughly half the battery capacity of the standard model: what did Honor trade, and for what? Start there and the Watch 6 Pro spec sheet reads clearly.

Honor opened European pre-sales on November 19. The Pro sits above the Watch 6, which launched globally on June 18, and it was announced in China at the end of September. No US release has been announced.
Sources: [TalkAndroid](https://www.talkandroid.com/535704-honor-watch-6-pro-europe-launch-price/), [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/11/19/honor-just-supercharged-its-smartwatch-lineup-with-a-new-pro-model/)

## 515mAh for 18 days, versus 980mAh for 35

|---|---|---|
| Display | 1.5" LTPO tandem AMOLED, 466×466, up to 5,500 nits | 1.46" AMOLED, 464×464, up to 3,000 nits |
| Battery | 515mAh | 980mAh (silicon-carbon) |
| Endurance claim | Up to 18 days (ultra-long mode) / 10 regular / 7 always-on | Up to 35 days (optimal conditions) |

Sources: [TalkAndroid](https://www.talkandroid.com/535704-honor-watch-6-pro-europe-launch-price/), [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/11/19/honor-just-supercharged-its-smartwatch-lineup-with-a-new-pro-model/), [Fonearena (Watch 6)](https://www.fonearena.com/blog/485354/honor-watch-6-price-features.html)

> **Not yet disclosed:** the Pro's chipset and OS, Korea availability and Korean pricing. Endurance figures are Honor's own and use different modes, so they are not like-for-like.

The Pro's battery is about 53% of the Watch 6's (515 ÷ 980). In return you get a larger, brighter screen, a ceramic bezel and a stainless frame, and about 9g more weight. The published numbers describe a nicer-looking watch, not a longer-lasting one. Because Honor quotes endurance under different conditions for each model, 18 versus 35 days is not a clean like-for-like gap.

## €279 is a pre-sale price that ends December 31

In Europe the black fluoroelastomer model lists at €379 (£329) and the silver steel-link model at €429 (£379). Through December 31 they drop to €279 (£229) and €329 (£279). Early buyers get a leather strap, clip-on earbuds and a 12-month warranty extension. The promo depends on stock and voucher availability.

At list price in the UK, the Pro costs about £80 more than the Watch 6's £249.99. That compares UK prices only; both models' euro prices were not confirmed in the same source.

## A China feature is missing from the European spec sheet

Honor promoted cardiac arrest detection in China, but it does not appear in the European press release or spec sheet, and Honor has not said why. Don't assume the China marketing list carries over.

## Korea availability is not confirmed

Nothing in the announcement covers a Korean launch or a won price. Converting euro prices would not be an official number, so we don't. If you're considering one, wait for an official launch.

## Who it fits

- Buying for screen brightness and materials: the Pro leads (5,500 nits, ceramic bezel).
- Putting battery life first: on published numbers, the Watch 6's 980mAh favors it.
