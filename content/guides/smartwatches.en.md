Brightness and battery are maker figures, not measured under one standard. Apple's 25 hours for the Ultra 4 is an extended workout mode with low-power settings, not continuous GPS, so it is kept out of the standard-use column. TBC under Korea means the launch material didn't confirm it.

## Start from your phone

- **iPhone**: Apple Watch is the only option here. The Galaxy Watch Ultra 2 is Android-only and won't pair. Satellite emergency SOS is on the Ultra 4 only; for everyday wear the Series 12 starts cheapest at KRW 599,000.
- **Android, battery and brightness first**: among models with both figures confirmed, the Galaxy Watch Ultra 2 leads (60 hours, 5,000 nits), and its Korean launch price is below the Ultra 4's.
- **Android with Google apps as they are**: the Pixel Watch 5 runs Wear OS with full Google services. A Korea launch was not confirmed.
- **Brightness and case material first**: the Huawei Watch 6 Pro's 3,500 nits and ceramic or titanium cases stand out, but it has no Google Mobile Services, so don't expect Google Maps or Assistant, and battery and water resistance haven't been disclosed.
- **A local Korean purchase**: in this table the Apple Watch Ultra 4 and Series 12 and the Galaxy Watch Ultra 2 have confirmed Korea availability. The rest are imports or waiting on a local release.
